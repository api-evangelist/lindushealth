# Lindus Health

> Lindus (Lindus Health) is a next-generation contract research organization (CRO) that runs
> end-to-end clinical trials for biotech and pharma sponsors, powered by Citrus™, an AI-assisted
> trial operating system. Lindus does not publish a public developer API.

properties and GitHub organization. Provider does not publish its own /llms.txt (probed

## What Lindus does

Lindus positions itself as an accountable, single-vendor alternative to the traditional
multi-vendor CRO model: one integrated team and one operating system from protocol design through
database lock, on performance-based contracts tied to milestone delivery. Founded by co-CEOs Meri
Beckwith (former life sciences VC) and Michael Young (former Special Adviser to the UK Prime
Minister). Over 160 staff.

Core capabilities:

- Citrus™ AI-assisted operating system — EDC, eSource, ePRO/eCOA, eConsent, participant
  pre-screening, CTMS, database, and real-time operational monitoring in one system. eSource
  supports automatic data abstraction and upload from EMRs.
- Centralized patient identification and enrollment — AI-powered EHR querying against a 40M+
  patient-record database, digital pre-screening, direct-to-patient campaigns, and centralized
  patient concierge support.
- Flexible site models — site-based, hybrid, or fully decentralized delivery across a preferred
  network of 1,000+ locations globally, plus virtual site staff (PIs, nurses, coordinators) and
  site augmentation.
- Integrated clinical services — feasibility, protocol development, project and site management,
  data management, through to database lock.

Therapeutic focus areas: cardiometabolic, respiratory, psychiatry, women's health, dermatology,
diagnostics.

## API and developer surface

- No public API. api.lindushealth.com, docs.lindushealth.com, and developers.lindushealth.com do
  not resolve in DNS.
- No OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL, or webhook documentation published.
- No /.well-known/ discovery documents, no security.txt, no status page, no trust center.
- Citrus™ is a customer-facing platform for sponsors and sites, not a self-serve developer product.

## Open source

- https://github.com/LindusHealth/django-sdtm-export — first-party Django library for exporting
  clinical model instances to CSV or CDISC Dataset-JSON following CDISC SDTM guidelines. Not
  released to PyPI; installs from source. Marked as under construction.
- https://github.com/LindusHealth/cdisc-rules-engine — Lindus fork of the CDISC open-source rules
  engine for clinical dataset conformance validation.
